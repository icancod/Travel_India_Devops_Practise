WHO WE ARE

Crafting Journeys That Leave Footprints on the Soul

Founded by a collective of cultural historians, wildlife preservationists, and luxury travel architects, Explore India was born from a desire to reveal India beyond superficial monuments.

We believe travel should be regenerative. That is why our itineraries intertwine grand royal palaces and serene backwaters with intimate village weaver collectives, classical temple dance recitals, organic spice farm visits, and community-led conservation sanctuaries.

HISTORICAL TIMELINE

Chronicles of India's Tapestry

From Bronze Age civilizations to the world's most vibrant modern democracy.

3300 BCE — 1300 BCE

Indus Valley & Vedic Dawn

Sophisticated urban sanitation in Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, foundational Sanskrit hymns, yoga philosophy, and astronomical observatories.

Foundational Heritage
3rd Century BCE — 12th Century CE

Golden Dynasties & Stone Marvels

The timeless rock-cut temples of Ellora, Ajanta murals, majestic Chola bronzes in Thanjavur, and Nalanda—the world's first residential university.

Monolithic Architecture
15th — 18th Century

Mughal Grandeur & Rajput Valour

Intricate marble inlay art of the Taj Mahal, hill forts of Rajasthan, courtly Hindustani classical music, and the Malabar spice trade routes.
